# Sharding Without Tears

Speaker: Otto Brambleworth, Mossgrove Analytics (fictional)

Sharding spreads a collection across several machines, and the shard key decides everything that follows. Otto explains how to choose a shard key with enough cardinality, why a monotonically increasing key creates a hot shard, and how the balancer moves chunks between shards. He finishes with the zone sharding setup a fictional retailer used to keep European customer data on European hardware.
