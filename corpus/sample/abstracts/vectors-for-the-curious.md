# Vectors for the Curious

Speaker: Mira Vantablack, Lumenfold Labs (fictional)

Numbers that capture meaning: Mira shows how text becomes an embedding, how nearest-neighbour lookup finds passages that mean the same thing even when they share no words, and where approximate indexes trade a little accuracy for a lot of speed. Live demo: building a question-answering helper over a fictional cookbook, and the three ways it confidently answered the wrong question.
